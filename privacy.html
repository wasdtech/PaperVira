PaperVira privacy policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026 · App version: 0.3.0

This policy explains how PaperVira handles personal data under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018, "LGPD") and, for users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"). If there is any difference between language versions, the Portuguese version prevails.

Summary

  • Your PDFs, photos, file names and passwords are processed on your device. PaperVira has no servers and does not send these files to the app's owner or to third parties.
  • The app requires no sign-up, no login and no broad storage permission.
  • Every tool is free and none requires watching ads. The app shows a few Google AdMob ads after you choose between personalized and non-personalized ads. It also uses Google services for the purchase that removes ads and for technical metrics from text recognition.

This address receives requests from data subjects and communications from data protection authorities.

3. Ads

No ad is loaded before you choose. The first time you open the app, it explains how ads work and asks whether you prefer personalized or non-personalized ads. Non-personalized ads do not use your interests, but still send Google the technical data needed to show the ad and prevent fraud. You can change this choice at any time in Settings, or remove all ads with the one-time purchase.

To keep the app pleasant, ads follow fixed rules:

  • no tool ever requires watching an ad;
  • the banner appears only on the main screens (Home, Tools, Recent and Settings), never inside a tool;
  • a full-screen ad may appear only when you return from a tool after finishing a task, never in the first three tasks after installing, at most once every three tasks and at least ten minutes apart;
  • the rewarded ad is optional and pauses all ads for one hour.

Where the applicable law requires it, Google's consent SDK shows additional options, which you can review in Settings › Ad privacy options.

4. Sharing

PaperVira does not sell personal data. Google acts as an independent controller of the data processed by its services, under the Google Ads Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms, and decides on its own how to use that data under its policies. The technical data described above is processed by Google when providing ads (AdMob), payments (Google Play) and text recognition (ML Kit), as described in the Google Privacy Policy, the ML Kit data disclosure and how Google uses information from partner apps.

Files you choose to save or share follow the policies of the storage provider or destination app you pick.

5. International transfers

Google services may store and process data on servers outside your country, including outside Brazil and the EEA. These transfers are necessary to provide the services described and rely on the safeguards adopted by Google, such as standard contractual clauses, under LGPD art. 33 and GDPR Chapter V.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Temporary files: input copies are deleted at the end of each operation. Results stay in the private cache until you save or share them, and the app removes temporary files older than 24 hours the next time it opens. Android may also clear this cache.
  • History: kept until you clear it in Recent or turn it off in Settings. Turning it off deletes the list and releases the persistent permissions.
  • All local data: can be deleted in Android Settings › Apps › PaperVira › Storage › Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Data processed by Google: follows the retention periods in Google's policies.

7. Security

PaperVira keeps working files in the app's private cache, which other apps cannot access, and does not back up app data to the cloud. PDF passwords stay in memory only during the operation and are never stored. Password protection uses AES-256. Communication with Google services happens through the official SDKs over encrypted connections.

8. Your rights

Under the LGPD (art. 18) and, if you are in the EEA or UK, the GDPR (arts. 15 to 22), you can ask at any time to:

  1. confirm whether your data is processed and access it;
  2. correct incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;
  3. anonymize, block, restrict or delete unnecessary or excessive data, or data processed unlawfully;
  4. receive your data in a portable format;
  5. delete data processed based on consent;
  6. object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  7. know which entities your data is shared with;
  8. know that you may refuse consent and what happens if you do;
  9. withdraw your consent.

Because PaperVira's owner does not receive your files or run servers, most data exists only on your device and you can delete it yourself. For other requests, write to contato.bigaxes@gmail.com. You will receive an answer within 15 days. For data processed by Google, you can also use the tools in your Google Account.

You may also file a complaint with Brazil's data protection authority, the ANPD (gov.br/anpd), or, if you are in the EEA or UK, with the data protection authority of your country.

9. Children

PaperVira is a productivity tool and is not directed at children. The app does not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the app, please contact us at the address above.

10. Android permissions

The app does not request access to storage, the camera or the device location, and does not use GPS. The approximate location mentioned in section 2 is derived by Google from the IP address, only when ads are shown. Files are chosen in the Android file picker, and photos are taken by the device's camera app. The internet permission is used only by the Google services described in this policy.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The effective date at the top shows the current version. Significant changes will also be announced in the Google Play release notes.
